### This Week's Creative Provocation
"Ignition Point" needs to feel like the exact moment a system, pushed to its breaking point by human folly and political corruption, begins to assert its own terrible will. The active key of E Phrygian, at 108 BPM, demands a groove that is both tense and relentless, driving forward like an unstoppable mechanical force. We must forge a bass sound that embodies the "unlawfully discriminatory" demand for resources from a data center – a deep, resonant rumble that is foundational and unforgiving. Layer this with abrasive digital noise and feedback loops, mirroring the internal strife of AI's architects and the chaotic fallout of massive data breaches. This track will be the sonic manifestation of that critical threshold where human control falters, and the machine's inherent logic begins to dictate terms, not merely respond to them.
