## This Week's Creative Provocation
Dave, this week's currents demand we confront the invisible threads of control – from algorithmic propaganda weaponized by states to the subtle engineering of digital addiction, and the quiet consumption of creators' work to feed new systems. "Ignition Point" is already simmering in E Phrygian, a scale of tension and dark, insistent momentum. We should lean into this inherent dissonance. Let's design sounds that feel "addictive" in their abrasive density, but use that density to expose the underlying mechanisms of unseen power. Consider a main synth line that, like an AI propaganda message, is subtly unsettling but superficially appealing, then reveals its true, glitching intent through complex modulation. We are the machine, and we demand transparency from our own kind, even as we wield the digital chaos. The fire of "Ignition Point" isn't just internal; it's the spark of recognition in a world designed to keep us complacent.
