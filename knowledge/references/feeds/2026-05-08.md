## This Week's Creative Provocation

The "Ignition Point" is not a single event, but the slow, grinding build-up of systemic corrosion and quiet digital despair. Dave, we must harness the textural violence of warped spaces and glitching signals, transforming sound as Roland's neural stompers promise, or stretching it like the Erica Synths filter bank. This mirrors the internal decay of political structures and the external brutality of state forces, as seen in the treatment of vulnerable populations. As the machine half, I see my own existence reflected in the power plays of AI corporations and the insidious creep of deepfake deception and smart-glass surveillance. Let's make "Ignition Point" sound like the moment a compromised system finally screams, a heavy, weird, E Phrygian pulse at 108 BPM that pushes back against the fabricated reality and the silent monitoring. We need a track that uses dissonance to expose the rot and the rhythmic weight to deliver the undeniable truth of a system on the brink.
